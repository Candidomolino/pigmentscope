PRIMARY PAPERS FOR PIGMENT COLOR ANALYSIS — checked 2 November 2026

Seručnik, Vicente, Brečko, Coutinho, Ventura & Žnidaršič-Plazl (2020). Development of a Microfluidic Platform for R-Phycoerythrin Purification Using an Aqueous Micellar Two-Phase System
https://doi.org/10.1021/acssuschemeng.0c05042
Primary page: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7737240/
Status: Inspected files
Images: Color photograph verified in Supporting Figure S4: pink coloration of the ultrafiltration membrane. Figure 4 in the article shows the microsettler at different flows.
Videos: AVI inspected: 480 × 368, 15 frames/s, 17.07 s. It is grayscale. Useful for flow/interfaces, unsuitable for native pigment hue.
Use: Best first color-image example. Membrane color is not a direct concentration or recovery measurement.
Reuse: Supporting files: CC BY-NC 4.0; retain attribution. Local photo is extracted and converted from JP2 to PNG; MP4 is a remux; the browser uses a VP9 lossless-encoded WebM derivative.

Luo, Smith, Raston & Zhang (2016). Vortex Fluidic Device-Intensified Aqueous Two Phase Extraction of C-Phycocyanin from Spirulina maxima
https://doi.org/10.1021/acssuschemeng.6b00756
Primary page: https://doi.org/10.1021/acssuschemeng.6b00756
Status: Inspected supplement
Images: Supporting Figure S5 contains experimental photographs, inspected and included. Embedded image is only 77 × 253 pixels and includes equipment.
Videos: Public supporting repository lists a PDF only; no video file was verified.
Use: Directly relevant continuous-flow/ATPS application. The small photographs are qualitative; equipment colors and compression confound pigment measurement.
Reuse: Supporting PDF: CC BY-NC 4.0. Included photograph extracted without color adjustment.

Boravelli & Vir (2024). Continuous flow aqueous two-phase extraction of betalains in millifluidic channel
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cej.2024.153265
Primary page: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1385894724047533
Status: Media access needed
Images: The primary abstract verifies beetroot extraction in a PEG/ammonium-sulfate millichannel. Full-resolution process photographs have not been inspected.
Videos: No supplementary video was verified. Do not assume there is one.
Use: Strong subject match for a future dataset. Obtain the full paper and supporting files through authorized access before treating its figures as analysis inputs.
Reuse: Access/reuse terms must be checked before redistributing media.

Stücheli, Sieber, Fuchs, Scheller, Strittmatter, Saxena, Gademann & Fussenegger (2020). Genetically encoded betaxanthin-based small-molecular fluorescent reporter for mammalian cells
https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaa342
Primary page: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7337513/
Status: Method verified
Images: Figure 4 and Supporting Figure S15 describe a smartphone setup and RGB analysis of yellow betaxanthin in a cuvette, including a blue-channel calibration curve.
Videos: No analysis-ready visible-color movie was verified.
Use: Best pigment-specific justification for calibration. This is a cuvette/reporter assay, not microfluidic extraction; its calibration cannot be transferred to unrelated published photographs.
Reuse: Primary article/repository record identifies CC BY 4.0; verify specific supplemental materials before reuse.
